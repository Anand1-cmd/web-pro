How to make fried Rice

What You Need

  • 2 eggs
  • 1 cup cooked rice
  • 2 tablespoons oil
  • 1 tablespoon soy sauce
  • 1 teaspoon garlic powder

Steps

  1. Crack the eggs and scramble them in a pan.
  2. Add the cooked rice to the pan and stir to combine.
  3. Drizzle the oil and soy sauce over the rice.
  4. Season with garlic powder and stir well.
  5. Cook for a few more minutes until everything is heated through.
